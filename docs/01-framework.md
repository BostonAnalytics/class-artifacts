# 1. The framework

## The idea in one line

Every module gets one short activity inside the lecture. The activity produces an **artifact**. Students attach the artifact to the discussion post they already write.

## Why an artifact

- **It connects the lecture to the discussion.** Students leave class with what the post needs, so posts are grounded in work they did, not a summary of the reading.
- **It is personal.** When every student works from their own question, their own data or their own version of a case, no two artifacts are the same. That makes posts hard to copy or to outsource to AI, and gives replies something real to compare.
- **It is visible.** You can see, in one place, what the class understood, and debrief it live.

## What counts as an artifact

Anything a student can attach or paste into a discussion post:

| Subject | Example artifact |
|---|---|
| Analytics or programming | A table of inputs and outputs, a short code snippet, a step count table |
| Management or strategy | A one-page decision memo, a ranked list with reasons |
| Finance or accounting | A filled-in worksheet, a small model with assumptions stated |
| Communication or humanities | A concept map, an annotated passage, a role-play summary |
| Science or health | A labeled diagram, a prediction with the evidence for it |

## The student rhythm

| Step | Where | What students do |
|---|---|---|
| **Learn** | During the lecture | Learn the concepts through the lecture content. |
| **Comprehend** | During the lecture | Try it out in the activity and generate an artifact. |
| **Demonstrate** | Discussion board | Submit the artifact in the discussion and compare with the class. |
| **Apply** | Beyond the module | Use it in future lessons, assignments, the term project and the real world. |

## Why it is easy to integrate

- **Nothing new to design.** No new learning objectives, assignments or grade categories.
- **No new submission.** The artifact reinforces the discussion post students already submit.
- **Any level of technology.** A web app, a form tool, or paper.
- **Works online.** Every step can be followed from the recording if students miss the live session.

## Three ways to take part, one artifact

| | Live in class | From the recording | Offline |
|---|---|---|---|
| When | During the activity segment of the lecture | Pause at the activity slide | Any time, with a printed sheet |
| Input | Unique per student | The same unique input | A versioned sheet or their own data |
| Submit | Artifact with the discussion post | Same | Same (a photo of the sheet is fine) |

Rules that make the activity work from a recording:

- Put the full instructions on the activity slide, not only in what you say.
- Use the same activity icon on every activity slide so it is easy to find while scrubbing.
- Never make the activity depend on live class results; post a snapshot of the results with the recording instead.
- List the activity timestamp in the recording description.
