# 5. No web app? Simple techniques for any course

Each technique ends in something students can post. That is all the framework needs.

| Technique | What students do | The artifact | From the recording |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Think, pair, share** | Answer alone, compare with a partner, then share with the room | The written answer | Compare with a sample partner answer you provide |
| **Paper activity** | Work through a printed sheet or case by hand | A photo of the sheet | Print or fill the PDF at home |
| **Role play** | Act out a real scenario in assigned roles | A short write-up of the decision | Take one role and write the decision alone, or pair up over video |

## Making inputs unique without software

- **Versioned sheets.** Make versions A to F of the same sheet with different numbers, names or cases. Students use the version that matches the last digit of their student ID (0 or 6 = A, 1 or 7 = B, and so on).
- **Their own material.** Ask students to bring something only they have: a question from their job, a decision they faced, data about themselves.

## Form tools

A form you already have (Microsoft Forms, Google Forms, an LMS survey) covers most of what the web app does:

- One form per activity, opened on the activity slide.
- Students download or screenshot their response as the artifact.
- You show the summary chart in class as the class results.

What a form does not do well: give each student a different input, or let students see a formatted artifact card. If you need those, use the starter web app.
