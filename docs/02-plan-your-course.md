# 2. Plan your course: 14 weeks or 6 weeks

The unit is the **module**: one learning objective you already teach, one activity, one artifact, one discussion. The calendar only changes how long a module lasts.

## 14-week semester (6 modules)

Each module spans two weeks (one class meeting per week). Weeks 13 and 14 are left for the term project and presentations. This is how MET AD 599 runs in Fall 2026.

| Weeks | Module | Activity (Comprehend) | Discussion (Demonstrate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | Module 1 | In the lecture where the objective is taught | Opens in week 1, artifact attached, due end of week 2 |
| 3 to 4 | Module 2 | Same | Opens week 3, due end of week 4 |
| 5 to 6 | Module 3 | Same | Opens week 5, due end of week 6 |
| 7 to 8 | Module 4 | Same | Opens week 7, due end of week 8 |
| 9 to 10 | Module 5 | Same | Opens week 9, due end of week 10 |
| 11 to 12 | Module 6 | Same | Opens week 11, due end of week 12 |
| 13 to 14 | Term project | Students reuse earlier artifacts (Apply) | Optional project reflection |

Notes:

- With two meetings per module, put the activity in the meeting where the module's main objective is taught, usually the first. The second meeting can debrief the class results.
- If your course has fewer forums than modules, attach two modules' artifacts to one forum, or make the last one a short project reflection.
- Seven modules of two weeks also fit, with no separate project weeks.

## 6-week summer term (6 modules)

Each module is one week. The pace is faster, so keep each activity to one artifact that takes 15 to 30 minutes.

| Week | Module | Activity (Comprehend) | Discussion (Demonstrate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Module 1 | In that week's lecture | Opens day 1, due end of the week |
| 2 | Module 2 | Same | Same |
| 3 | Module 3 | Same | Same |
| 4 | Module 4 | Same | Same |
| 5 | Module 5 | Same | Same |
| 6 | Module 6 | Same | Same, or folded into the final project |

Notes:

- In a 6-week term, students who watch the recording have less slack. Post the recording and the class results snapshot within 24 hours.
- If initial posts are due mid-week and replies at the end of the week, ask for the artifact with the initial post.
